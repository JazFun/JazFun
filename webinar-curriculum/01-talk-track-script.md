## PHASE 2: THE FRAMEWORK REVEAL [0:13 - 0:25]

**[0:13 - INTRODUCE THE 3-TIER MODEL]**

"I call this the **3-Tier AI Operating System Architecture.**

Most people think about AI tools in terms of: 'ChatGPT versus Claude' or 'This prompt versus that prompt.'

That's the wrong frame.

The right frame is: **What tier are you operating in?**

Let me show you what I mean."

**[0:14 - TIER 1: TASK LAYER]**

"**Tier 1 is the Task Layer.** This is where most firms live.

Task Layer means:
- Individual prompts for individual problems
- Custom GPTs that do one thing well
- Quick wins that save time on specific tasks

Examples:
- A GPT that formats client emails
- A prompt that summarizes long documents
- ChatGPT helping you write a policy

This is valuable. This is where everyone starts. But here's the limitation:

**Tier 1 is fragile.**

It depends on individual people knowing the right prompts. It doesn't scale. It doesn't transfer. When Sarah leaves, her prompts leave with her.

Tier 1 gives you efficiency. But it doesn't give you a system."

**[0:17 - TIER 2: WORKFLOW LAYER]**

"**Tier 2 is the Workflow Layer.** This is where the jump happens.

Workflow Layer means:
- AI that holds your processes, not just executes tasks
- Systems that give consistent guidance to your whole team
- Intelligence that applies YOUR logic to new situations

Examples:
- A Claude Project that holds your SOPs and answers questions about how your firm operates
- A workflow assistant that guides new hires through your month-end process
- An operations brain that applies your decision rules consistently

Here's what changes at Tier 2:

**Your team can execute without you.**

Not because they memorized everything. But because the system holds your logic and delivers it consistently.

This is where repeatability lives. This is where you stop being the bottleneck."

**[0:20 - TIER 3: SYSTEM LAYER]**

"**Tier 3 is the System Layer.** This is the operating system.

System Layer means:
- Unified intelligence across multiple functions
- Brand voice, operations, HR, client service—all connected
- Verification and quality control built in
- Your firm runs on systematized intelligence, not individual judgment calls

At Tier 3, you're not asking 'How do I use AI for this task?'

You're asking 'How does my firm's operating system handle this situation?'

**This is where the gap-wideners live.**

They're not doing more with AI. They're architecting their firms differently."

**[0:22 - THE REVELATION]**

"Here's the key insight:

**Most firms stay at Tier 1 forever.** They keep collecting prompts. They keep building isolated GPTs. They get efficiency gains but never build a system.

**The jump to Tier 2-3 isn't about learning more tools.** It's about changing how you think.

You stop thinking like a tool user.
You start thinking like a systems architect.

That's what I'm going to show you how to do in the next 25 minutes.

We're going to build one thing from each tier. You'll see exactly how the progression works. And you'll leave knowing how to replicate this for your firm.

### BUILD #3: SYSTEM LAYER - VERIFICATION FRAMEWORK [0:38 - 0:50]

**[0:38 - SET UP THE REAL PROBLEM]**

"Build number three. Tier 3. System Layer.

Here's the concern I hear from every firm owner who's gotten this far:

'I love this. But I'm nervous. What if the AI gives my team bad advice? What if they just do what Claude says without thinking?'

**AI doesn't make decisions alone. AI makes recommendations that go through your verification framework.**

That's what we're going to build right now. And this is what separates a tool from an operating system."

**[0:40 - THE VERIFICATION LAYER CONCEPT]**

"Here's the principle:

Claude gives guidance. Your team doesn't just execute it. They run it through a verification checklist first.

If all checks pass, they execute. If any check fails, they escalate.

This keeps YOU in control while letting your team move fast.

Let me show you exactly how this works."

**[0:41 - BUILD THE FRAMEWORK LIVE]**

"[STAY IN CLAUDE - Same project or show document]

I'm going to show you the verification checklist that goes WITH the Operations Brain.

For any guidance Claude gives, before the team acts on it, they answer five questions:

**Verification Checklist:**

1. **Contract Alignment:** Does this comply with our client engagement terms?
2. **Timeline Impact:** Does this affect any deadlines or deliverables?
3. **Approval Required:** Does this decision need manager/partner sign-off?
4. **Client Communication:** What needs to be communicated, and by whom?
5. **Documentation:** Where does this get logged?

[SHOW OR BUILD THIS LIST]

Now let's apply it to what Claude just told us."

**[0:44 - APPLY THE VERIFICATION]**

"Remember Claude's guidance on the client wanting to change their reporting date?

Let's run it through:

1. **Contract Alignment:** Does changing from the 15th to the 5th violate our engagement letter?
   - Answer: Check the engagement letter. If it specifies dates, we need client sign-off on amendment.

2. **Timeline Impact:** Does this affect our month-end workflow?
   - Answer: Yes, significantly. The 5th is tighter. We need to assess capacity.

3. **Approval Required:** Can the team member approve this?
   - Answer: No. Date changes require manager approval per our escalation policy.

4. **Client Communication:** What do we tell the client?
   - Answer: 'We're reviewing the request and will confirm by [date].'

5. **Documentation:** Where does this go?
   - Answer: Client file notes + workflow calendar update.

[WALK THROUGH EACH ONE - 2 minutes]

See what just happened?"
