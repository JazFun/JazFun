### **SEGMENT 5: WHY FIRMS FAIL (AND HOW TO WIN) (10 min)**
*Delivers on Promise #4*

**[60:00-70:00] The Adoption Failure Pattern**

**Why 80% of Firms Fail at AI Adoption:**

1. **No clear use case** → "We bought Copilot but no one uses it"
2. **Leader uses it, team doesn't** → Creates bottleneck, not scale
3. **Fear of errors** → Perfectionism kills progress
4. **No feedback loop** → Can't improve what you don't measure
5. **Shiny object syndrome** → New tool every week, mastery of none

**The Exact System for Full Team Adoption:**

```
Week 1: Leader masters ONE workflow
Week 2: Document the workflow (video + template)
Week 3: Train ONE team member
Week 4: Team member trains the next
Week 5: Workflow is team standard
Week 6: Add second workflow
```

"Adoption isn't a training event. It's a 6-week installation."
